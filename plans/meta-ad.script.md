**Status:** Full restart per Nick 2026-07-13. v1–v4 (mock-UI/kinetic-type approach) are in git history; Nick judged the mock look not good enough. From here on the ad is cut from real videos Nick uploads. Remotion does the cutting, type, timing, and brand styling , not the pixels.

## The core idea (Nick's words, keep this framing)

> Constantly switching between tabs, not even a response. After spending hours finding the job, emailing people in the office, and filling out the application.

The pain isn't the busywork itself. It's the **silence after the effort**: hours of tab-switching, finding the role, emailing people at the company, filling out the application , and then… nothing. No response. That beat ("all that → nothing") is the hook. Scout is the answer to the silence, not just the workload.

## Working structure (loose until footage arrives)

1. **The grind**: real screen recordings: tab-switching, job hunting, emailing, the application form.
2. **The silence**: the empty inbox / "no response" moment. Land it hard.
3. **The turn**: let Scout take over.
4. **Proof**: real Offerloop product footage doing the work.
5. **Close**: get your time back / offerloop.ai.
