Offerloop glass badge

The disc is translucent and blurs whatever sits behind it, so it picks up the colour of the surface it lands on. Scroll through the backgrounds below: it is the same markup every time.

On the carousel mesh

Its home ground. The 48px one uses the tighter 0.74 mark.

On a photograph

The second raises the white veil to 0.52. Over a busy or saturated background the default 0.36 lets too much through and the mark loses contrast.

On dark

The default veil survives on dark but goes muddy, since the navy half of the mark is competing with a dark disc. The second pushes the veil to 0.82, which turns the glass back into a light plate and gives the mark its contrast back.

On flat paper

Nothing behind it to blur, so the glass collapses to a pale disc and only the rim and shadow give it edges. This is exactly what the baked alpha PNG looks like, and the reason it exists as a warning rather than a default.
